Seven waves locked.
Three to go, and one wire missing.

Waves 1 through 7 are complete and gated. Sixty-three modules were declared across the ten-wave plan; forty are locked behind a check that fails the build when they drift, seven are partial, three are absent, one was retired by the pivot, and twelve are not yet due.

The platform builds, serves and refuses correctly. 109 routes exist, 107 pages are generated from the route table, and 19 constitutional gates plus 735 tests pass on every run. Authentication landed this session and the office authority model with it.

What stands between this and a live product is not code. No database is provisioned, so the magic link, the grant table and the sixteen-stage passport have nowhere to persist. Everything downstream of that — Wave 8's approvals and notifications, Wave 9's cognition, Wave 10's sovereign tier — is waiting on the same connection string.

Information Architecture

109 routes, generated from one table

Nothing in app/ is hand-written. Every page, every route-group layout, the 404 and the 500 are emitted by gen-app.js from constants/routes.ts, and app:check fails the build when the tree and the table disagree. A URL exists because the route table says so — a hand-added page would be a surface with no declared access class, reachable and invisible to every check written to govern reachable things.

The v5 migration completed this cycle: the old 135-route v3 tree is gone, 110 permanent IA ids are unique, and constants/redirects.ts now serves 110 301s through next.config.ts — a table that had been written but wired to nothing.

Findings

What would stop a launch

Blocker · 01

No database is provisioned

Two migrations are written and neither has been applied. This single absence blocks the email magic link (a verification token must be written down before it can be checked), the grant table, the sixteen-stage passport, notifications, search and every Wave 8 module.

Google sign-in is the exception and works without it — an OAuth handshake with a JWT session needs no adapter.

migrations/0000_productive_scorpion.sql institutional schema — never applied migrations/0001_naive_puck.sql 5 auth tables — never applied DATABASE_URL unset
Blocker · 02

Nothing is committed

171 tracked changes plus the new auth layer exist only in the working tree, on main, level with origin/main. The last commit is the v4 workbook. A stray checkout loses the entire v5 migration and this session's work.

Risk · 03

Rate limiting will not survive serverless

lib/rate-limit.ts is an in-memory map, and its own comment concedes the problem: on serverless, instance memory is per-instance. The two public lead endpoints are the only unauthenticated write paths in the system and they are effectively unthrottled across instances.

Risk · 04

Accredited and member are not derivable

Both return false and will keep returning false. Accreditation is the recorded outcome of PR-01; the Member Law fires on settlement, not on sign-in. Twenty of the 109 routes are unreachable by anyone until those institutional records exist — which is correct behaviour, not a bug, but it means "sign in and see your portfolio" is not yet a journey the platform can complete.

Risk · 05

One critical and four high advisories

npm audit reports a critical in vitest and a high in vite — both development-only. The next high, inherited through postcss and sharp, reaches production and should be resolved before a public deploy.

Closed this cycle

Six defects found and fixed

The v5 route migration completed and app/ now matches the canon. Beyond that: /404 and /500 had been dropped from the route table, so the generator removed both files as orphans and production fell back to Next's stock pages — the one place a stack trace could still reach a visitor. sitemap.ts was advertising retired v3 chapter URLs and /roles/* to crawlers. robots.ts disallowed five dead v3 paths while the live private namespaces went unlisted. Three workspace previews were indexable and in the sitemap. And the grant paths shipped earlier this session bypassed the GP-06 separation check that LAW 2 requires — now enforced on the resulting right set, by every path that writes.
