02 / Detailed lifecycle

Ten connected stages.

The following is a target workflow map. It preserves the known command boundaries and identifies the integration and operating flows that need implementation underneath them.

01 / SETUP

Form the vehicle and its operating perimeter.

Office creates the legal wrapper, links it to the organization, registers the property candidate, appoints authorised roles and establishes the initial project and document registers.

02 / COMMITMENT

Investor self-KYC and illustrative ₹50,000 commitment.

Investor completes a self-directed identity and eligibility flow, uploads required evidence, selects an offering and enters ₹50,000 as an illustrative first subscription amount. The system validates completeness, offering capacity and the accreditation window; it does not self-grant accreditation or self-accept a binding commitment.

GC consumes a minimal authorised status projection: allocated, available, exception or completed—never a shadow booking system.

04 / Boundary rules

Automate the route. Never fabricate the outcome.

OFFLINE IS A FIRST-CLASS STATE

Legal execution, banking and reconciliation are represented as pending/confirmed evidence states. They are not faked as completed because a person submitted a form.

TIME IS AN INTEGRATION

The external Time platform manages allocation. GC holds entitlement logic and receives only the authorised projection necessary for the holder relationship.

MEMBERSHIP FOLLOWS SETTLEMENT

A commitment does not create a member. Settled capital creates an ownership position, which creates the holder projection and associated rights.

PUBLIC CAPACITY IS A PROJECTION

Publicly visible available units decrease after accepted/settled state according to disclosure policy. Private identity and transaction detail never leak through capacity messaging.

DOCUMENTS AND ACTIVITY ARE SHARED ROOTS

One document and one event record serve many views. Disclosure policy changes the projection, not the underlying evidence.

COMMUNICATION FOLLOWS DECISION

Notifications and content explain authorised events. They do not substitute for a resolution, approve an exception or alter a financial record.
