Strategy / not a screen list / source-led composition

Wireframe the operating truth.

Every wireframe begins with a governed workflow, not a visual idea. The public surface creates desire; the private surface builds conviction; the member surface makes ownership legible; the Office surface executes the work. One Vehicle truth moves through four apertures without being restated.

01 / StartChoose one lifecycle moment and named command—not a generic page.
02 / MapDeclare aperture, object, evidence, authority, next state and owner.
03 / ComposePlace cinematic or functional DNA around the one job of the screen.
04 / TestProve empty, loading, blocked, exception, permission and completed states.
01 / Wireframing law

Five rules before drawing.

Use these as a hard entry checklist. A frame that lacks an object anchor, an authorised action or an exit state is not ready for visual design.

W-01

One job

Every screen answers one primary question and offers one primary next action.

W-02

One truth

Apertures redact and re-order the Vehicle record; they never create alternate facts.

W-03

Evidence first

Consequential claims, documents and numbers expose source, date and status.

W-04

Authority visible

Write controls declare who may act, what is required and why a control is unavailable.

W-05

Mode follows risk

Cinematic carries desire. Functional carries orientation, evidence and decision control.

02 / The repeatable wireframe method

Use the same six passes for every packet.

Do not design the polished frame first. Begin with the operational skeleton, then tune the visual pressure only after the workflow can be completed safely.

PASS 01
Workflow slice

Name trigger, actor, lifecycle gate, object and completion event.

Screen inventory

List entry, review, evidence, confirmation, exception and exit screens.

Output

One-page workflow map with no visual styling.

PASS 02
Disclosure map

Mark Public, Investor, Member or Office aperture and sensitive fields.

Authority map

Map read/write scope, named owner, approval rule and external confirmation.

Output

Screen annotations and disabled/blocked control states.

PASS 03
Information hierarchy

Place the decision-critical fact first; supporting evidence second; help third.

Component assembly

Choose hero, chapter rail, evidence break, docket, timeline, table or action frame.

Output

Greyscale low-fidelity frames at desktop and mobile.

PASS 04
State coverage

Draw empty, loading, complete, exception, external-pending and no-permission variants.

Motion & accessibility

Apply cinema only to arrival; shutter/settle to controls; specify reduced-motion fallback.

Output

Annotated interaction/state sheet.

PASS 05
Evidence review

Check every number, action and document has an object/evidence reference.

Workflow review

Walk the frame with the accountable owner and a counterparty representative.

Output

Approved wireframe packet or explicit gap docket.
