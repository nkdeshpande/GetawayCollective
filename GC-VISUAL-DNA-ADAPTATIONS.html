04 / Motion language

One system of movement.
Two tempos.

Motion establishes the mode before copy does. It is never a substitute for orientation, and it always respects reduced-motion preferences.

Cinematic reveal

600ms · cinema curve. Used for image crops, chapter reveals and a considered arrival into the visual world.

Functional response

120–240ms · shutter curve. Used for chapter navigation, document access, status transitions and any decision-adjacent control.

05 / Assembly law

Build pages from the same instruments.

C-01 · CINEMATIC HERO

Full-bleed place image. One statement. No data over the image.

C-02 · BLACK PAUSE

Void section that lets the preceding image settle before another claim begins.

C-03 · IMAGE CHAPTER

One anchor frame plus three details. Every frame declares its type.

C-04 · CHAPTER RAIL

Only Functional mode uses persistent navigation through the asset record.

C-05 · PAPER EVIDENCE BREAK

Paper means a checkable assertion: material, stage, document or rule.

C-06 · INVITATION FRAME

One quiet final route. Never urgency, scarcity theatre or a forced decision.

06 / Non-negotiables

Two adaptations.
Never two brands.

INVARIANT 01

Geometry

Zero radius. No shadows. Depth comes from void, paper, blur and the scale of images.

INVARIANT 02

Type

Outfit for authority and visual statements, Inter for reading and Space Mono for evidence. No serif rendering appears in either adaptation.

INVARIANT 03

Colour

Copper belongs to capital. Electric belongs to action and state. Forest identifies the organic asset world.

INVARIANT 04

Truth

Public desire never impersonates private evidence. Every figure and document appears at the correct disclosure stage.
