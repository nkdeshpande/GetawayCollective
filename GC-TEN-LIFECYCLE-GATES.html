GC.SYSTEM / TEN LIFECYCLESLG-01 · GATE SPECIFICATION
Taxonomy / prerequisites / workflow / evidence / exit conditions

Ten lifecycle gates.

These are controlled operating lifecycles, not page labels. A gate must be explicitly satisfied, evidenced and authorised before the next stage opens. The structure distinguishes public visibility, private materials and an open capital Offering—three different states that must never be collapsed.

G0 / Required parentObject and legal relationship exist.
G1 / Evidence completeRequired source material is attached and current.
G2 / Authority decisionNamed scope and conflict check allow the command.
G3 / External confirmationBank, counsel, registry or Time platform confirms its own state.

02 / Property visibility and Offering gates

Do not confuse a property page with an offer.

A public Property profile can create awareness before an Offering exists. Private materials can be disclosed before capital is accepted. An open Offering is the final, more heavily gated state.

Offer taxonomy

Mode A

Pre-acquisition

Raise subject to acquisition conditions. No claim that land is owned.

Mode B

Acquisition

Land/control is secured or closing is imminent under documented terms.

Mode C

Development

Land is held; permission and delivery conditions are disclosed.

Mode D

Stabilised

Operating asset; trading and reserve evidence become relevant.

Required legal-policy decision

Land possession, title completion and permissions are not presently universal code gates. This specification requires the Investment Committee and legal counsel to ratify which of the four offer modes is permitted, its condition precedents, its permitted claims and its required risk disclosure before any Offering may open.

Exit condition

All required documents are effective, funds are cleared and reconciled, and the legal admission basis is confirmed. A payment screenshot or a completed web form is never settlement evidence.

10 / Board, resolutions and communications

Decide once. Communicate the authorised result.

Meetings and communications are separate but connected: a board packet assembles evidence and open dockets; a resolution records the decision; a communication projects the permitted outcome to the right audience.

Exit condition

The lifecycle closes only when the authorised result, audience-appropriate communication and completion evidence are all linked. A meeting note or a broadcast message is not itself a completed governed action.
