## **Information Architecture Re-Architecture Directive v4.0**

### **Desire → Consideration → Commitment → Membership → Wealth Stewardship**

---

# **1\. THE MANDATE**

Rearchitect Getaway Collective as a **desire-led private real-estate investment and membership platform**, not as an investment administration portal.

The platform must accomplish two very different jobs without allowing either to weaken the other.

**Before investment**, Getaway Collective must create emotional and social desire around exceptional real assets.

**After investment**, Getaway Collective must transform into an extraordinarily capable private wealth and ownership interface through which a Member understands, controls and experiences their relationship with every Getaway Collective LLP.

The transition must feel continuous.

Do not create:

> Marketing Website → Investment Portal → Back-office Dashboard

Create:

> **Desire → Discovery → Understanding → Conviction → Commitment → Ownership → Stewardship → Belonging**

The public experience should seduce.

The investment experience should clarify.

The Member experience should reassure, empower and delight.

The underlying system must remain constitutionally rigorous throughout.

---

# **2\. DO NOT CHANGE THE CONSTITUTIONAL MODEL**

The underlying domain remains:

```
                         LLP
                  Constitutional Vehicle

                         │
             ┌───────────┼───────────┐
             ▼           ▼           ▼

           SPACE        TIME       CAPITAL
              \          │          /
               \         │         /
                 GOVERNANCE
```

This remains the canonical truth architecture.

SPACE answers:

> What does the LLP own?

TIME answers:

> What annual allocation does ownership create?

CAPITAL answers:

> What is the financial position of the LLP and Member?

GOVERNANCE answers:

> Under what legal, contractual and fiduciary rules does everything operate?

Do not expose this ontology prematurely to a prospective investor.

It becomes progressively visible as the relationship deepens.

# **42\. PRODUCT INVARIANTS**

Ratify these.

### **UX-01 — Desire Before Structure**

The public journey leads with the asset and its meaning before investment mechanics.

### **UX-02 — Truth Never Changes With Vantage**

Public, Investor, Member and GC views may present different authorised projections, but canonical facts remain singular.

### **UX-03 — Progressive Disclosure**

Complexity increases only when intent requires it.

### **UX-04 — Place Remains Visible**

Financial ownership never becomes detached from the underlying physical asset.

### **UX-05 — Explainability**

Every consequential financial, ownership and governance fact must be explainable through authorised evidence.

### **UX-06 — Continuity**

No Member should repeat information already known and lawfully available to GC.

### **UX-07 — Human/AI Continuity**

IRIS → Human → IRIS transitions preserve context.

### **UX-08 — No Administrative Leakage**

Internal GC organisational complexity must not leak into the Member experience.

### **UX-09 — No Dark Patterns**

No artificial scarcity, hidden risk, manipulative urgency or suitability implication.

### **UX-10 — Stewardship Before Cross-Sell**

Existing holdings and Member obligations always take priority over new opportunities.

### **UX-11 — Context Before Documents**

Documents appear within the object or decision they evidence.

### **UX-12 — Intelligence Before Navigation**

Where the system can reliably bring the correct information or action to the Member, it should not require the Member to hunt for it.

## **LONG-FORM JOURNAL COPY**

The city teaches us to measure time in increments.

Nine-thirty.

Eleven.

One-fifteen.

Six-thirty.

Twenty minutes to the airport.

Thirty minutes until the next call.

Four minutes late.

We become extraordinarily good at measuring time while becoming increasingly poor at experiencing it.

The coast behaves differently.

There is morning.

There is high sun.

There is rain approaching from somewhere you cannot yet see.

There is the hour when the heat begins to leave the ground.

There is sunset.

There is darkness.

The clock remains.

It simply becomes less important.

Perhaps this is one reason people have always been drawn to the edge of land.

The horizon removes information.

There are fewer buildings to read. Fewer signs. Fewer intersections. Fewer objects demanding interpretation.

In one direction, almost nothing.

Sea.

Sky.

Weather.

Distance.

The mind has less to process.

And slowly, the day begins to feel larger.

### **The luxury of an unimportant hour**

Much of modern luxury is designed around addition.

Another course.

Another room.

Another amenity.

Another destination.

Another thing to do.

But some of the most valuable hours are valuable precisely because very little happens inside them.

Coffee that becomes breakfast.

A walk without a destination.

A book abandoned halfway through because someone has fallen asleep.

Children moving between water and shade.

Rain cancelling whatever was supposedly next.

An afternoon that cannot be described afterwards as productive.

We increasingly optimise our working lives to remove empty time.

Then spend money trying to find it again.

### **Return changes a place**

There is another difference between visiting somewhere and returning somewhere.

Novelty asks for attention.

Familiarity gives attention back.

The first time, you discover where the light falls.

The fifth time, you already know.

The first time, you search for the road.

Later, the turn arrives before you think about it.

A destination gradually stops being a destination.

It becomes part of the geography of your own life.

This is where SlowSpace Coastal begins as an idea.

Not with accommodation.

Not with an itinerary.

With **return**.

A project-specific LLP has been formed around a proposed coastal property in Nadsal Village, Kaup Taluk, Udupi District.

The legal structure is important.

The economics are important.

The architecture will be important.

But none of those explains why a coastal place should exist in someone's life in the first place.

The first question is simpler.

> **What would you do with a day that felt longer than it was?**

## **JOURNAL COPY**

Parents often talk about childhood as though it disappears suddenly.

It doesn't.

It disappears incrementally.

One summer at a time.

One school year.

One birthday.

One trip they can attend.

Then one they cannot.

For years, the family calendar feels endless.

Then children acquire calendars of their own.

The mathematics is uncomfortable because it is so ordinary.

There are only so many long weekends when everyone is available.

Only so many school holidays when children still want to go wherever their parents are going.

Only so many years between carrying someone into the sea and watching them walk ahead of you.

We tend to think about family wealth in monetary units.

Assets.

Education.

Inheritance.

Security.

But families accumulate another kind of capital.

**Repeated experience.**

And repeated experience needs somewhere to happen.

### **Memory likes repetition**

Ask someone about the most expensive hotel they visited at eight and they may remember almost nothing.

Ask about a house they returned to every year and the details can be extraordinary.

The smell after rain.

Where the towels lived.

The dangerous step.

The tree someone always climbed.

The particular breakfast.

The walk to the water.

The chair a grandparent always occupied.

Memory does not necessarily reward spectacle.

It rewards recurrence.

A place becomes meaningful because life happened there repeatedly.

### **Why ownership changes the psychology**

Travel is usually organised around novelty.

Where haven't we been?

Ownership asks another question.

Where do we want to return?

This distinction matters.

A place you expect to see again is experienced differently.

You notice change.

The children notice change.

You develop rituals.

You stop trying to consume the destination.

Eventually the place begins accumulating family history.

### **The inheritance before inheritance**

We usually imagine inheritance happening after us.

Perhaps some of the most valuable inheritance happens while everyone is still here.

A vocabulary of places.

Shared rituals.

A coastline everybody knows.

Stories that begin:

“Remember when we were there…”

SlowSpace Coastal is being developed around a coastal property in Udupi District.

Its eventual value will be measured in financial terms.

It will have land, buildings, operating economics, maintenance, debt where applicable, reserves and governance.

Those things matter enormously.

But they are not the only form of value a place can hold.

Some returns never appear on a balance sheet.

They appear twenty years later around a dining table.

The property identified for SlowSpace Coastal measures approximately **29 cents** in Nadsal Village, Kaup Taluk, Udupi District.

Every decision matters.

### **Coast is not a view**

It is tempting to treat coastal architecture as a visual problem.

Orient building toward sea.

Create opening.

Frame horizon.

Finished.

But coastal conditions are physical.

Salt.

Humidity.

Wind.

Rain.

Water table.

Heat.

Corrosion.

Drainage.

Maintenance.

The current SlowSpace Coastal design intent specifically contemplates deep micro-piling and sheet-piling as responses to the high coastal water table.

Water supply is currently conceived around a 3.5-bar booster system rather than conventional rooftop tanks.

These are not the parts of architecture that usually appear in photographs.

They are often the parts that determine whether the photograph still looks good ten years later.

### **Social without surrendering privacy**

The current design intent also contemplates a separate coastal social component.

The proposed Z2 Coastal Plaza includes a social deck and a subterranean preparation kitchen intended to separate operational noise and heat from the principal social space.

The larger idea is more interesting than the programme.

**Can a property participate in its surroundings without making the people staying there feel publicly exposed?**

The current concept attempts to physically separate public/day-use activity from private accommodation.

That boundary—between social energy and retreat—may become one of the project's most important architectural problems.

### **Architecture as stewardship**

When a property is held through an LLP, architecture acquires another responsibility.

The building is not merely an aesthetic object.

It becomes an asset that must be:

maintained;

insured;

operated;

repaired;

valued;

improved;

and eventually transferred.

A beautiful detail that fails every three monsoons is not beautiful enough.

A material that photographs perfectly but cannot age in coastal conditions is not resolved.

Architecture therefore becomes part of capital stewardship.

SlowSpace Coastal is still a project in formation.

That makes this the right moment to document it.

Not after every difficult decision has been erased by finished photography.

But while the questions are still visible.

# **Canonical numbering law**

The IDs should **never be recycled**, even when a page is retired.

Use:

```
GC-     Enterprise/Public
INV-    Investor
MEM-    Member
OFF-    Office
SPA-    Space
CAP-    Capital
TIM-    Time
PRJ-    Project
PAR-    Partners
GOV-    Governance
DOC-    Documents
ACT-    Activity
NET-    Network
SYS-    System
```

Numbers advance in increments of 10:

```
CAP-100
CAP-110
CAP-120
```

so later insertions can use:

```
CAP-115
```

without renumbering the architecture.

**The ID is permanent. The URL is not the ID.**

This matters because routes can change without destroying references from requirements, analytics, permissions, tests and documentation.

There is one especially important design rule here: **do not create `/office/partners`, `/office/capital`, `/office/projects`, `/office/assets` as primary navigation destinations.** Those concepts only become meaningful to GC when scoped to an Investment Vehicle. Cross-vehicle analysis belongs in **Collection** or **Network**, not in a second competing object hierarchy.

The result is a very disciplined mental model:

> **Enter GC → choose the Investment Vehicle → understand its Space, Capital, Time and Governance → manage its Project and Partners → verify through Documents → understand change through Activity.**

That should now be the backbone for the **route manifest, RBAC matrix, Unified Field Registry, Next.js App Router folders, analytics event taxonomy and sitemap/access-control rules**—all derived from the same permanent IA IDs rather than designed independently.
