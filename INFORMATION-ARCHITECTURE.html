The rules this table runs on

Six laws.

access Is Derived

A route's access comes from its assembly's vantage. The aperture tier already decided what each vantage may see, and a route restating it would be a second source of truth.

overrides State Their Reason

An override without a reason is a permission granted by whoever edited the file last.

no Super Admin

The eight roles are offices and committees, not tiers. A super-admin holds every right, which is the exact condition separationViolations() exists to detect. Where the highest authority is genuinely needed, the route requires a resolution reference rather than a role.

rights Not Roles

Admin routes name the RIGHT they require, never the role that holds it. Rights are granted and revoked; a route bound to a role would survive the revocation.

index Only Public

An indexed URL behind authentication leaks its existence and usually its title. Only public routes are indexable, and the linter enforces it rather than trusting the default.

not Found Never Confirms

403 says the viewer may not see it and never says whether it exists. The difference between 'no' and 'not for you' is the shape of the system, handed to anyone probing it.
