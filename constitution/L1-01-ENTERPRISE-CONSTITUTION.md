# PART I — ENTERPRISE IDENTITY

## 1. What Getaway Collective Is

Getaway Collective is a **Private Real Estate Investment Platform**.

It acquires, structures, governs, and manages exceptional experiential real estate on behalf of its investors.

**The Core Function**

Getaway Collective is an institutional capital manager. It:

- **Acquires** properties meeting constitutional investment criteria
- **Structures** them into investment vehicles (funds, SPVs, trusts)
- **Governs** them with transparent decision-making and institutional oversight
- **Manages** them for capital preservation, cash flow, and long-term appreciation
- **Appoints** operating partners to deliver member experiences
- **Reports** performance, risk, and valuation to investors

**What Getaway Collective Does NOT Do**

Getaway Collective does not:

- Operate hospitality (Sensory Getaways does)
- Deliver concierge services
- Manage guest journeys or check-in
- Own operational SOPs, guest satisfaction, or F&B

These belong entirely to Sensory Getaways, an operating company appointed by the investment platform.

**The Constitutional Test**

Would an **investment committee** discuss this? If yes, it belongs in Getaway Collective. If no, it belongs in Sensory Getaways or outside the platform entirely.

Examples:
- Property valuation ✔ (Investment Committee)
- Capital call ✔ (Investment Committee)
- Guest breakfast ✗ (Operating Review)
- Studio housekeeping ✗ (Operating Review)

# PART II — THE SOVEREIGN QUARTET

The enterprise is composed of exactly **four sovereign domains**. This is the foundational abstraction. No fifth domain may be created without constitutional amendment.

| Domain | Ontology | Primary Question |
|---|---|---|
| **ASSET** | Scarcity | What exists physically, and where is it? |
| **IDENTITY** | Agency | Who is allowed to act, under what authority, and with what accountability? |
| **FINANCIAL** | Value | Who owns economic rights, how does value flow, and how do decisions legally change outcomes? |
| **TIME** | Allocation | How many nights is each Member entitled to? |

## 8. The Fundamental Law

```
ASSET     creates opportunity.
IDENTITY  performs action.
FINANCIAL assigns consequence.
TIME      defines entitlement.
```

Expressed as composition:

```
Scarcity (ASSET) × Agency (IDENTITY) × Allocation (TIME) = Value (FINANCIAL)
```

## 9. Domain Sovereignty

Each domain owns its truth absolutely and may not reach into another.

**ASSET owns:** physical reality, structural definition, lifecycle evolution, temporal availability, usage constraints, operational condition, environmental context.
**ASSET does not own:** ownership, identity, payments, governance outcomes.

**IDENTITY owns:** actor existence, authentication state, authority definition, accreditation and eligibility, session legitimacy, accountability and audit, abuse protection, administrative control.
**IDENTITY does not own:** assets, money, governance outcomes, economic rules.

**FINANCIAL owns:** legal wrappers, ownership definition, share classes and rights, capital formation, governance voting power, distribution logic, fee structures, valuation state, liquidity mechanisms, economic disclosure.
**FINANCIAL does not own:** physical reality, actors, authentication, operational execution.

## 10. The Separation Law

```
FINANCIAL decides WHO may use.
ASSET     decides WHEN usage is possible.
IDENTITY  decides WHO is acting.
```

Any feature that collapses two of these three into one implementation is unconstitutional.

## 11. Time Is a Domain — RATIFIED 30 Jul 2026

**TIME is the fourth sovereign domain.**

TIME tracks the scarcity of nights (temporal allocations) that a Member receives as part of an Ownership Interest in a Property. TIME is the domain that converts investment (FINANCIAL) into usage rights (IDENTITY) within a physical asset (ASSET).

**Constitutional consequences:**

- TIME is a **first-class sovereign domain**, carrying equal ontological weight to ASSET, IDENTITY, and FINANCIAL.
- TIME tracks **entitlements**. It answers: "How many nights does this Member own in this Property?"
- TIME does **not** track real-time scheduling, blocking, or reservation state. That occurs in L4 (State & Lifecycle) and L5 (Capabilities) as operational state, not as a domain-level phenomenon.
- SYSTEM is **not** a domain. It is L11 (Infrastructure & Runtime).
- The Four Quadrants (§28) now operate over four domains: SPACE (ASSET), CAPITAL (FINANCIAL), TIME, and MEMBER (IDENTITY).

## 24a. Governance Thresholds — RATIFIED

*Ratified 30 Jul 2026. Source: EP-01 §3.8 Voting Framework. Supersedes BLANK-08.*

### Basis of Voting Rights — RATIFIED 30 Jul 2026

> **Voting rights are proportional to the percentage equity stake held in the vehicle.**

Voting is **equity-weighted, not per-capita**. A partner holding 7% of an LLP casts 7% of that LLP's voting rights. There is no one-partner-one-vote mechanism at any threshold.

Three consequences follow, and each is load-bearing:

1. **The concentration cap is also a governance cap.** Because no holder may exceed 10% of any property or 10% of the aggregate portfolio (§27), no single holder can command more than 10% of the vote. A blocking position against a Special Resolution (>24%) therefore requires at least three coordinated holders.
2. **Quorum is an equity measure.** The 60% quorum means partners representing 60% of *equity*, not 60% of *partners*.
3. **Entrenchment means 100% of equity.** Unanimity under §32b is unanimity of total voting rights — an absent partner is a missing consent, not an abstention. A single holder of any size can block an entrenchment amendment.

**Equality within class.** Voting rights attach uniformly to equity within the same ownership class. Two partners holding identical stakes in the same class hold identical rights — this is an entrenched principle (§32b).

**Voting rights attach to ownership, not to accreditation status** (§24b). An expired accreditation does not suspend a vote.

### Legal Vehicle

Until otherwise approved by Constitutional Amendment, every investment vehicle established by Getaway Collective shall be constituted as a **Limited Liability Partnership (LLP)**. No alternative vehicle (private limited company, trust, REIT, corporate partnership) may be established without Board approval and constitutional review. Each LLP maintains independent accounts, statutory filings, partner registers, financial statements, and governance records.

| Threshold | Value | Applies To |
|-----------|-------|-----------|
| **Ordinary Resolution** | **> 50%** of voting rights present | Annual budgets · committee appointments · operating policies · routine governance |
| **Special Resolution** | **≥ 76%** of voting rights | Constitutional amendments · material asset acquisition/disposal · admission of new partners · removal of designated partners · ownership structure changes · borrowing beyond approved leverage · merger/dissolution/restructuring of an LLP · related-party transactions above materiality threshold · amendments to management, operating, or brand participation agreements materially affecting investor economics |
| **Quorum** | **≥ 60%** of total voting rights | All meetings. Failure to achieve quorum requires adjournment and reconvening per the LLP Agreement. |

**Proxy Voting.** Permitted. A proxy shall be in writing, relate to one meeting only unless otherwise specified, may not be re-delegated, and shall be retained with meeting records.

**Tie Resolution.** A tied resolution is deemed **NOT APPROVED**. The burden of approval rests with the proposer. No casting vote exists, except where expressly provided in the LLP Agreement for administrative matters that do not affect investor rights, capital allocation, or governance.

**Engineering note:** These three constants (`ORDINARY = 0.50` exclusive, `SPECIAL = 0.76` inclusive, `QUORUM = 0.60` inclusive) are the only voting thresholds in the system. `voting.ts` must expose no others.

## 24b. Accreditation — RATIFIED 30 Jul 2026

### Validity Period

Accreditation issued by Getaway Collective is valid for **fifteen (15) working days** from the date of approval.

Accreditation facilitates a **specific investment transaction**. It does not constitute permanent investor eligibility. Each investment opportunity requiring accreditation requires a current and valid accreditation at the time of commitment.

### Process

Accreditation is an **offline compliance process** conducted by the Enterprise Compliance Function. It may include, as appropriate to the opportunity: identity verification · KYC and AML verification · financial suitability assessment · investor documentation · legal eligibility review · source of funds verification · jurisdictional compliance · any additional information reasonably required.

> **The specific assessment methodology is intentionally not prescribed in this Constitution.** A constitution defines outcomes, not managerial discretion. Methodology is governed by the Enterprise Compliance Policy and Accreditation Standard.

### Renotification

Because validity is only fifteen working days, **no mandatory constitutional notification schedule is prescribed**. The Enterprise may issue courtesy reminders through operational procedures. **Failure to issue a reminder does not extend the validity of an accreditation.**

### In-Flight Actions on Lapse — COMPLETE-THEN-SUSPEND

Where an investment commitment has been **formally accepted and entered into the execution process** while accreditation remained valid, the transaction may proceed to completion notwithstanding subsequent expiry.

> **Expiry occurring after lawful execution does not invalidate an otherwise valid commitment.**

Immediately following completion:
- the member is classified **Accreditation Expired**;
- no additional commitments, subscriptions, transfers, or capital actions may be initiated until accreditation is renewed.

**If accreditation expires *before* the enterprise has formally accepted the commitment, the commitment lapses automatically** and a new accreditation is required before any further investment activity.

**Reasoning.** The legal eligibility test applies at the **point of acceptance**, not during subsequent settlement mechanics. This avoids transaction uncertainty while ensuring no *new* investment activity proceeds under an expired accreditation.

### Lapse Management

Upon expiry: new investment commitments prohibited · participation in new LLP offerings prohibited · transfer requests requiring accreditation suspended · **existing ownership interests unaffected** · previously executed contractual rights continue per the LLP Agreement.

### Governance Rights Survive Lapse

> **Voting rights attach to ownership, not to accreditation status.**

A member whose accreditation has expired continues to exercise all governance, voting, distribution, and information rights attached to their existing LLP interests, unless otherwise restricted by law, the LLP Agreement, or a final regulatory determination.

Accreditation governs **eligibility to make new investments**, not ownership rights.

### Escalation

Where exceptional circumstances require an expired accreditation to remain under review, the matter is referred to the Enterprise Compliance Function, which may recommend an exception to the Executive Office where necessary to protect legal compliance · investor interests · transaction integrity · enterprise obligations. **All exceptions documented in the Compliance Register.**

### Jurisdictional Variance

These constitutional principles apply **uniformly across all jurisdictions**. Jurisdiction-specific documentary requirements, verification procedures, eligibility tests, and regulatory obligations are administered through the Enterprise Compliance Policy.

**Where local law imposes a higher standard than this policy, the higher legal standard prevails.**

## 31. Constitutional Failure — RATIFIED 30 Jul 2026

### Constitutional Principle

A **Constitutional Failure** occurs where the Enterprise materially fails to comply with this Constitution in a manner that compromises fiduciary responsibility, investor rights, governance integrity, or constitutional invariants.

> **A Constitutional Failure is a governance event, not an operational incident.**

Operational failures become Constitutional Failures only where they expose a failure of constitutional governance, fiduciary duty, or institutional controls. A bug is not a constitutional failure. A bug that reached production because the governance controls designed to catch it did not operate — that is.

### Trigger Conditions

Declared upon the occurrence of **any one** of:

| ID | Trigger | Definition |
|----|---------|-----------|
| **CF-01** | **Constitutional Invariant Breach** | A constitutional invariant is violated in production — knowingly or unknowingly — in a manner materially affecting investors, governance, ownership, financial integrity, or enterprise obligations |
| **CF-02** | **Distribution Integrity Failure** | Any distribution executed to an incorrect recipient · in an incorrect amount · outside the approved distribution waterfall · in breach of the LLP Agreement · without required governance approvals |
| **CF-03** | **Fiduciary Breach** | Misuse of investor funds · undisclosed related-party transactions approved · material conflicts of interest undisclosed · acting contrary to investor interests · knowing breach of constitutional governance |
| **CF-04** | **Governance Failure** | A material governance decision made outside delegated authority · without required approvals · without quorum · contrary to constitutional voting requirements · using materially false or misleading information |
| **CF-05** | **Regulatory or Legal Failure** | A material breach of law or regulation resulting from failure to comply with constitutional governance or enterprise policy |
| **CF-06** | **Asset Stewardship Failure** | The enterprise knowingly permits material deterioration of an LLP asset through unapproved deferred maintenance or neglect resulting in measurable loss of asset value or investor interests |

### Authority to Declare — AMENDED 30 Jul 2026

**Interim authority (current state).** Until the Governance & Ethics Committee is constituted, a Constitutional Failure is declared by the **Getaway Collective executive team — the Chief Operating Officer or the Chief Executive Officer**.

**Standing authority (on constitution of the Committee).** Declaration authority transfers automatically to the **Governance & Ethics Committee**. No amendment is required for this transfer; it occurs on the Committee's first sitting, recorded in the Governance Register.

**Recusal in both states.** Where the alleged failure involves the declaring authority itself, the declaration is made by an **Independent Constitutional Review Panel** appointed by the Board.

> ⚠️ **Recorded weakness — accepted knowingly.**
>
> Executive declaration means that in the interim state, the officers most likely to be implicated in CF-03 (Fiduciary Breach) and CF-04 (Governance Failure) are the same officers empowered to decide whether a failure occurred. This is the specific risk that committee declaration exists to remove.
>
> Three controls partially offset it, and none of them fully:
> - the recusal rule above, which depends on the executive correctly identifying itself as implicated;
> - the Constitutional Failure Register, which records declared failures but cannot record undeclared ones;
> - the Board's power to appoint an Independent Constitutional Review Panel on its own motion.
>
> **The residual exposure is a failure that is never declared.** Nothing in the interim structure detects that. Constituting the Governance & Ethics Committee is the mitigation, and it should be treated as time-bound rather than indefinite.

### Appeal

**The declaration itself cannot be vetoed or ignored.**

The Board may appeal the *classification* only on the basis that the constitutional criteria have not been met. Such an appeal shall:

- be lodged within **fifteen (15) business days**;
- include written reasons;
- be reviewed by the Independent Constitutional Review Panel; and
- **not suspend** any immediate remediation actions required to protect investors or enterprise assets.

**The Independent Constitutional Review Panel's determination is final.**

### Consequences

Upon declaration:

1. the affected constitutional layer is identified;
2. corrective actions commence immediately;
3. a **Constitutional Failure Report** is prepared;
4. root-cause analysis is completed;
5. affected policies, procedures, and controls are reviewed;
6. the affected constitutional layer is re-ratified where required.

**Re-ratification window:** ninety (90) calendar days from declaration, unless extended by the Board for exceptional reasons recorded in the Constitutional Failure Register.

### Disclosure

Material Constitutional Failures affecting **investor rights, financial reporting, distributions, ownership interests, or enterprise governance** shall be disclosed to affected LLP Partners within a reasonable period following confirmation.

Internal governance failures with no material investor impact may remain internal but shall still be recorded in the **Constitutional Failure Register**.

## 33. L2 Business Objects: The Institutional Investment Model — RATIFIED 30 Jul 2026

Getaway Collective is an **investment platform**, not an operating company. The L2 Business Objects reflect institutional real estate management, not hospitality operations.

**This is the complete and exhaustive L2 Business Object layer.**

### Enterprise Domain

| Object | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Organization** | Legal entity (Getaway Collective, GP, LP, subsidiary) |
| **Investment Vehicle** | Capital container (Fund, SPV, Trust, Syndicate, LLP) |
| **Portfolio** | Curated collection of Properties (e.g., Coastal Portfolio, Wellness Portfolio) |

### Assets Domain

| Object | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Property** | Investment-grade real estate asset (the investment target) |
| **Acquisition** | Process and record of acquiring a Property |
| **Valuation** | Fair value assessment (independent appraisal, NAV, cap rate, appreciation) |
| **Disposition** | Process and record of selling or exiting a Property |

### Capital Domain

| Object | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Investment Offering** | Regulated capital opportunity (Series A, Growth Round, Rights Issue) |
| **Investor** | Legal entity with capital commitment (Family Office, HNWI, Institution, NRI) |
| **Commitment** | Investor's binding promise of capital to a Vehicle (precedes investment) |
| **Capital Call** | Request: "We need ₹X million by Date Y" |
| **Investment** | Actual deployed capital (immutable record) |
| **Ownership Position** | Investor's current economic interest (computed; changes over time) |
| **Distribution** | Cash returned to investors (Dividend, Return of Capital, Profit, Exit Proceeds) |

### Governance Domain

| Object | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Agreement** | Legal instrument (SPA, SHA, Subscription Agreement, Operating Agreement, Management Agreement, Lease) |
| **Resolution** | Board or investor vote and its outcome |
| **Policy** | Constitutional rule (governance threshold, reserve floor, amendment procedure) |
| **Committee** | Governance body with decision authority |
| **Compliance Event** | Audit finding, regulatory notice, or breach record |

### Performance Domain

| Object | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Performance Report** | Quarterly/annual investor reporting (IRR, MOIC, NAV, cash yield) |
| **Benchmark** | Target or comparable (market return, portfolio average, peer fund) |
| **Forecast** | Projected performance (3yr, 5yr, exit scenario) |
| **Risk** | Risk register entry (liquidity, interest rate, operator, market, climate, currency, legal) |

### Intelligence Domain

| Object | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Due Diligence** | Pre-acquisition investigation and documentation |
| **Investment Thesis** | Why this Property should outperform over 20 years |
| **Market Intelligence** | Market data, trends, competitive position |
| **Research** | Topic-specific analysis (ESG, geographic, asset class, economic) |
