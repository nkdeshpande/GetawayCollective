01 / Write law

Automation is not an authority bypass.

Every workflow below follows the same boundary. Automation may collect, validate, draft, route, remind and publish a projection; it may not quietly change a governed fact, move capital or make a legal decision.

01 / SELF-DIRECTED

A person completes their own profile, preference, material request or enquiry. The system validates and records the submission.

02 / AUTOMATED

The system verifies format, detects missing evidence, creates a task, sets reminders, expires time-bound states and publishes safe projections.

03 / GUARDED

A person starts the action, but authority, object state, conflict and evidence gates decide whether the write may complete.

04 / RESERVED

Capital deployment, distributions, legal formation, resolution outcome and policy approval require named authority and retained reason.

03 / Self-serving workflows

Automate the path.
Reserve the decision.

These are the high-value flows that should reduce repeated operational handling without weakening the authority model.

01

Private materials

Request → identity continuation → eligibility check → correct disclosure package → viewing record → unanswered-question route.

02

Qualification

Guided upload → field validation → missing-item chase → review queue → timed accreditation → expiry notice.

03

Commitment

Scenario → terms acknowledgment → availability check → commitment draft → authority acceptance → funding instructions.

04

Property evidence

Partner upload → media classification → source/date check → milestone relation → approval queue → correct audience projection.

05

Governance

Resolution preparation → authority check → notice → sealed ballot → aggregate tally → immutable outcome.

05 / Design conclusion

Make routine work invisible.
Make material decisions explicit.

The product should feel self-serving because it remembers, validates, gathers, routes and explains. It should never feel casual about ownership, capital, authority or evidence.
