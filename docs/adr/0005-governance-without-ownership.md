# ADR-0005 - GC holds no equity in the vehicles it governs

**Status:** Accepted, 30 Jul 2026. **Reverses earlier constitutional text.**
**Authority:** L1-01 §27

## Context

§27 previously read: *"The platform retains a strategic ownership position
in every collection to align long-term incentives with members."*

## Decision

**No.** Getaway Collective shall not subscribe for, acquire or beneficially
own equity in any LLP it establishes, governs, administers or manages.

Compensation comes only from Board-approved, disclosed fees.

## Rejected

Co-investment. It has a real alignment argument - GC eating its own cooking,
which institutional LPs often want to see. It was rejected because every
investment decision would then be a related-party transaction requiring an
independent review mechanism, and the conflicts framework would carry that
weight permanently.

## Consequences

- Investment decisions are **not** co-investment decisions. No independent
  co-investor review is needed.
- Because GC holds no equity, **every** GC economic relationship with a
  vehicle runs through a service agreement. The related-party materiality
  threshold is therefore load-bearing across the entire fee model, not an
  edge-case control.
- The earlier text is repealed in place with an explicit reversal notice
  rather than quietly overwritten. An amendment register needs to show what
  was displaced.
